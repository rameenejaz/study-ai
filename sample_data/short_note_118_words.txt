Cloud computing delivers computing services such as servers, storage and software over the internet. Instead of buying hardware, organisations rent resources and pay only for what they use.

There are three main service models. Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) provides virtual machines and storage. Platform as a Service (PaaS) provides a managed environment for building and deploying applications. Software as a Service (SaaS) delivers complete applications through a web browser.

Elasticity is the ability to add or remove resources automatically as demand changes. This makes cloud systems cost-effective for workloads that vary during the day.

The main risks are data security, vendor lock-in and dependence on a reliable network connection, so companies must plan before migrating critical systems.
